## 📌 Project Overview

**Netflix Data Analytics & Business Insights using Python** is a comprehensive data analysis project developed as part of my **Data Analysis using Python Internship**.

The project analyzes Netflix Movies and TV Shows data to identify meaningful patterns, trends, distributions, and business insights. The complete workflow covers **data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, data visualization, and insight generation** using Python.

The analysis focuses on content types, genres, ratings, countries, release years, directors, cast, and content duration.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

* Analyze and understand the Netflix dataset.
* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
* Identify and handle missing values and duplicate records.
* Compare Movies and TV Shows.
* Analyze content ratings and audience categories.
* Identify popular genres and content categories.
* Analyze country-wise content distribution.
* Study release-year and content trends.
* Analyze directors and cast information.
* Examine movie durations and TV Show seasons.
* Create informative data visualizations.
* Generate meaningful business insights from the data.

## 📊 Analyses Performed

### 🎬 1. Movies vs. TV Shows Analysis

Analyzed the distribution of Netflix content by comparing the number of:

* Movies
* TV Shows
* Overall content distribution

This provides an understanding of the composition of the Netflix content library.

### 🎭 2. Genre Analysis

Analyzed Netflix titles based on their genres and categories to identify:

* Most common genres
* Popular content categories
* Distribution of different genres
* Differences in genre representation

### ⭐ 3. Rating Analysis

Analyzed content ratings to understand:

* Most common ratings
* Distribution of titles across ratings
* Audience-oriented content patterns

### 🌍 4. Country-wise Analysis

Performed country-level analysis to identify:

* Countries contributing the most Netflix content
* Geographic distribution of titles
* Major content-producing countries

### 📅 5. Release-Year Trend Analysis

Analyzed Netflix content according to release year to identify:

* Content trends over time
* Growth patterns
* Years with higher content representation
* Differences between Movies and TV Shows

### 🎥 6. Director & Cast Analysis

Explored director and cast information to identify:

* Frequently appearing directors
* Recurring cast members
* Creator contribution to the Netflix catalog

### ⏱️ 7. Content Duration Analysis

Analyzed:

* Movie durations
* TV Show season counts
* Common duration patterns
* Differences between Movies and TV Shows

### 📈 8. Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed EDA to discover relationships, patterns, distributions, and trends within the dataset using statistical methods and visual exploration.
